# Gucky model sheet

The approved Gucky: turnaround (front, side, back, side facing left), six expressions (neutral,
happy, surprised, worried, determined, sleepy) and a six-phase walk cycle, drawn by
`series/gucky/kit.js` at 1440×2560.

Approved by the user as drawn (PNG sha256
`0de9504993066cd528da8aab0612c90ef3b391e6243844433711189e9116a021`, Chromium). A simplified
variant was tried and rejected. After any rig change, re-render with
`node still.mjs ../stills/gucky-model-sheet/index.html --at 0 --out ../out/gucky-sheet-a.png`
and compare: a different hash means Gucky changed.

Not yet checked in Firefox. The back view's tail tuft sits high, near the neck.
