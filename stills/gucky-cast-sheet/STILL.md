# Gucky cast sheet

The four hatchlings side by side (Pip, Dot, Bean, Gucky), the hatching stages (speckled egg,
cracked and pipped, cap lifting, Gucky out wet in his cap), the nest with eggs sitting in it,
Mom as a hatchling sees her, and Gucky at his smallest (`s` .32) and a large close size (1.15).

Mom was redrawn in the ducklings' own vocabulary (scalloped edge, stick legs, rounded pad feet)
after the user pointed out that thick tube legs and spiky toes broke the style. The pip follows a
public-domain photo of pipping skimmer eggs: a small star of cracks round a hole.

The user approved the cast and asked for distinct personalities: each sibling now stands in its
resting look (Pip confident, Dot attentive, Bean sleepy, Gucky neutral). Not checked in Firefox.
