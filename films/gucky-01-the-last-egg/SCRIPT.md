# Gucky, Episode 1: The Last Egg (script, draft 3)

Status: draft for the user's approval; not built. Draft 3 rewrites Gucky's struggle after the
user's note on draft 2 and the cut: "Gucky struggle wasn't funny". It now gets its comedy from
character (he learns to walk by copying his siblings, each copy going wrong in that sibling's
way), escalates in three, pays off Dot's kindness, and calls back in the meadow. Draft 2 followed
the user's notes on the first full cut: the siblings leave the nest and go off screen; a second scene has them walking across while
the music matches the walk; Gucky stumbles and loses pace; then the boot slams down. The stumble
in the nest should look more natural, and the nest scene loses the water at the top: the pond is
where they are walking to, and it comes later.

Wordless. Vertical 9:16. Two scenes, about 36.5 s. Times are approximate until built.

## Scene 1: The nest, early morning

*Fixed camera, a little above the ducklings. A low straw nest in the lower middle; grass running
back to a soft horizon with a line of far bushes; open sky. No water.*

**0:00** Four eggs in the nest. Pip's, on the left, is already cracking and shivering.
*Sound: two sharp cracks.*

**0:00.3** PIP's egg squeezes down and fires its top into the sky. PIP rockets straight up after
it, hangs at the top in a hero pose, drops feet first, lands hard and shakes himself dry. He
preens and looks round for applause. Nobody claps.
*Music: a harp run up, a big plucked chord on the pose, a thud on the landing, drops on the shake.*

**0:02** DOT's lid flips open. A small DOT peeks out, sees PIP, ducks back down shyly, peeks
again, looks the other way, then hops out and shimmies dry in a little sparkle. She smiles.
*Music: a soft note on each peek; a glockenspiel sparkle.*

**0:04** BEAN's egg wobbles and falls over. BEAN slides out fast asleep, snores once, then dries
off in one enormous yawn.

**0:05.5** Three heads turn to the last egg: big, speckled, silent. Nothing happens. PIP blinks.
*Sound: silence, but for one tiny plink on the blink.*

**0:07.4** PIP marches up to it, knocks twice with his bill, then presses his head to the shell
to listen. The egg rocks back and bonks him over. He lands on his back, legs kicking: "hey!" A
crack runs halfway across the shell. DOT sees it first.

**0:11** PIP taps his foot, impatient, slower each time. The egg shivers. A tiny hole; a bill tip
peeks out and vanishes. Three pushes run the crack all the way round. The egg presses down.
*Sound: tremolo strings building under the pushes, then silence.*

**0:14.3** POP. The top flies off spinning; GUCKY tumbles out, wet and spiky, and lands on his
feet. The top comes down on his head: bonk. It stays there.
*Music: a plucked chord and a glockenspiel spark; a harp run up and down with the flying cap.*

**0:15** Nose to nose with PIP: who are you? GUCKY blinks at the world and gives his first peep,
very small. DOT smiles.

**0:16.6** MOM calls from off screen, right. Every head turns.

**0:17** PIP marches off to the right, chest out, knees high. BEAN trundles after him in a
sleepy side-to-side waddle. DOT stays, watching GUCKY.

*Gucky learns to walk the way babies do, by copying, and each copy goes wrong in that sibling's
way. Real newborn moves (wobbling on one leg, sitting back, swaying), timed for laughs: a still
beat and a blink after each one.*

**0:17.4** GUCKY copies PIP. He puffs out his chest and lifts one foot high, like a hero.
Holds it. Holds it. Wobbles on one leg. The weight of the cap tips him slowly backward until he
sits down on his tail: plop. A beat. He blinks. DOT giggles.
*Music: a proud two-note fanfare on the pose, a long note wobbling and sliding down, a soft plop;
Dot's giggle peep.*

**0:18.6** He gets up and copies BEAN's waddle: side to side, side to side. Each sway is bigger
than the last, until the last one swings him half round. He is facing the wrong way.
*Music: a marimba tick-tock that swings wider and slower; a swish on the turn.*

**0:19.6** Pleased with himself, he marches off, the wrong way, and steps straight into his own
empty eggshell. He clomps two steps wearing it like a boot, stops, and looks down at it. He
shakes his foot; the shell spins away across the nest.
*Sound: two hollow clomps, a rattle, a ting as the shell lands.*

**0:20.6** DOT, laughing, trots over and turns him round with a nudge of her bill. She heads off
after the others, looks back once and cheers him on with two peeps.

**0:21.4** Facing the right way at last, GUCKY sets his brow. Three careful steps, and each one
lands. He stops, looks down at his feet, amazed, then up, beaming.
*Music: the first three notes of his motif, one per step; a glockenspiel sparkle on the smile.*

**0:22.8** He runs off to the right after them, over the rim of the nest.

**0:23.6** The nest, empty but for the shells. A beat.

## Scene 2: The meadow, low along the ground

*The camera at duckling eye level, side on; the ground along the lower third; tall grass and a
few wildflowers; a big sky above. Everything moves left to right, toward the pond, still off
screen. The camera tracks with the line, the grass sliding past behind them.*

**0:24.1** PIP, BEAN and DOT march into frame from the left in single file, close together, in
step.
*Music: the walking theme begins: a light pizzicato march, one plucked bass note per step, a
glockenspiel tune on top. It bounces the way they walk.*

**0:26.1** GUCKY scrambles in from the left, catches up and skips into step at the end of the
line.
*Music: his motif joins the march, complete for the first time. He's in.*

**0:26.6 to 0:29.6** The four march together. GUCKY's cap bobs. He smiles.

**0:29.6** Proud to be in the line, GUCKY puffs out his chest and high-steps like PIP, and,
just like before, tips slowly backward onto his tail. Plop. The line marches on without him.
*Music: the march keeps time; his fanfare and the sliding note come back, out of step.*

**0:30.6** He scrambles up and hurries, his steps out of time with the music. The gap to DOT
grows: one length, two.

**0:32.1** SLAM. A huge rubber boot comes down out of the sky into the gap: BOOM.
*Sound: the music cuts dead; the stomp; dust.*
GUCKY skids to a stop at the toe. His cap jumps.

**0:33.1** Beyond the boot, unaware, the line marches on out of frame.
*Sound: their peeps, fading.*

**0:33.6** Silence. GUCKY's eyes go up. Then his head: up, up the boot, to a trouser leg that goes
on out of the top of the frame.

**0:35.1** *Music: his motif asks its question. No answer.*

**0:36.1** Hold on his face. END.

- The pond appears only at the end of episode 2 (Mom slides into it); this episode walks toward
  it.
- The boot's toe points the way the family went: in episode 2 Gucky decides the boot is Mom and
  follows it.
- Kept from the current build: everything to 0:16.6 (the hook, the siblings' hatches, the knock,
  Gucky's hatch), the orchestra and the motif. Changed: no water in scene 1; the siblings leave
  off screen right; Gucky's first steps reworked (draft 2 made them natural, draft 3 makes them
  funny: copying Pip, then Bean, then the wrong way into his own shell, turned round by Dot); the
  meadow scene is new, and his stumble there is a callback to copying Pip; the boot moves into it.
